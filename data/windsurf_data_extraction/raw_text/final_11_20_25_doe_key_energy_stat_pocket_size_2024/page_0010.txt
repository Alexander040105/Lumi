Environmental Emission Indicators
GHG emission is expressed in carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) 
which accounts for the global warming potential (GWP) of 
methane (CH4) and nitrous oxide (N2O), as prescribed by the 
Inter-governmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC). GWP is 
the ratio of the warming resulting from the emission of one 
kilogram of a greenhouse gas to that of one kilogram emission 
of CO2 over a fixed period of time (i.e. CH4 and N2O GWP is 21 
times and 310 times the CO2 emission, respectively).
