This issue presents an analysis of energy supply and demand situation 
in the Philippines for 2024 vis-à-vis 2023. The energy data used herein 
are based on the Energy Balance Table (EBT) (as of 15 June 2025) as 
generated by the Policy Formulation and Research Division (PFRD) of 
the Energy Policy and Planning Bureau (EPPB), unless otherwise stated. 
Kindly note that Non-Energy Use is included in the discussion for Total 
Final Energy Consumption (TFEC) in this report.
