A. Total Final Energy Consumption by Fuel
Oil and petroleum products continued to dominate the TFEC with an aggregate share 
of 50.8 percent, as their utilization increased by 4.9 percent from its year-ago level of 
18.8 MTOE to 19.8 MTOE (Figure 3). The uptrend reflected the country’s reliance on 
petroleum products, especially for transport which accounted for 65.5 percent share 
of the total oil demand in 2024, up by 5.1 percent from its 2023 level of 12.3 MTOE. 
The Services sector notably augmented its consumption of oil and petroleum products 
with an 11.3 percent of increase in 2024 and 11.9 percent share to the oil demand mix.

On the other hand, consumption of biomass such as fuelwood, charcoal, and 
agricultural residues stood at 7.3 MTOE in 2024, which represented 18.9 percent of the 
energy demand mix during the year (Figure 4). Despite its continued use among low-
income and off-grid households for traditional cooking, overall biomass consumption 
grew modestly by 0.9 percent. Establishments and industrial facilities made up the 
remaining share of biomass utilization, but their growth remained modest at only 0.7 
percent and 0.3 percent, respectively.
