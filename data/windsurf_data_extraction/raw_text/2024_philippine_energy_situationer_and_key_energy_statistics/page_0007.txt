The Philippines ranked among the fastest-growing ASEAN economies in 2024 with 
a 5.7 percent expansion in Gross Domestic Product (GDP), which was slightly behind 
Vietnam (7.1 percent) and ahead of major peers such as Indonesia (5.0 percent) and 
Malaysia (4.4 percent)1. Economic growth stemmed from increased household spending, 
continued momentum in the services sector, and favorable investor sentiment.

percent from last year’s 401.6 kTOE due to greater reliance on mechanized farming

the largest energy consumers in absolute

energy use. Transport grew by 5.2 percent, 
driven by rising vehicle ownership, expanded 
mobility services, and higher freight demand.
