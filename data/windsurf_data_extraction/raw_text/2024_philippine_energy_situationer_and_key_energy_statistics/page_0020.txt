v.	 Wind
Wind energy fell by 5.3 percent to 106.5 kTOE in 2024 compared to 112.5 kTOE in 2023. 
While it accounted for 0.2 percent of the energy mix during the year, offshore wind 
(OSW) development remained on the upswing. As of December 2024, 291 OSW service 
contracts have been issued which are expected to expand the country’s current 
installed capacity of 426.9 MW20.
vi.	Biofuels
In 2024, local production of biofuels continued to rise in response to increased 
demand for transport fuels and compliance with Biofuels Act of 2006. Biodiesel 
production reached 218.8 kTOE or 17.6 percent higher than its year-ago level, while 
bioethanol output increased by 1.2 percent to 216.6 kTOE. These expansions reflected 
the operational performance of twelve (12) biodiesel production facilities with a 
combined annual capacity of 615.3 million liters per year (MLPY)21, as well as twelve 
(12) bioethanol plants, with an aggregate capacity of 438.0 MLPY22.

Combined import volume of crude and finished petroleum products in 2024 slowed 
down by 2.1 percent compared to the 4.9 percent increase registered in 2023. Total oil 
imports reached 22.9 MTOE, as finished petroleum products accounted for 73.4 percent 
and crude oil contributed the remaining 26.6 percent. Crude oil imports fell by 4.5 
percent from 6.4 MTOE in 2023 to 6.1 MTOE during the year, attributed to constrained 
domestic refining, geopolitical tensions and regional economic uncertainties. This 
contraction was offset by the 4.6 percent hike imports of finished petroleum products, 
which emerged as a more economically viable solution, and helped sustain requirement 
of the transport sector, specifically for diesel and gasoline.
