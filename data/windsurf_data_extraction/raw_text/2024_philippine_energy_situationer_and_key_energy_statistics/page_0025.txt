The Philippine economy continued to show resilience and steady momentum, posting 
a slightly faster gross domestic product (GDP) growth of 5.7 percent in 2024 compared 
to the previous year’s 5.5 percent. Despite this modest acceleration, the country 
ranked as the second fastest growing economy in the region – next to Vietnam and 
ahead of its other regional peers23. This underscored the domestic economy’s ability 
to sustain growth amidst persistent external challenges, including subdued global 
demand, climate-related disruptions, and geopolitical tensions reminiscent of 2023.
The sectoral gross value added (GVA) of industry and services remained the strongest 
engines of growth for the year, expanding by 6.7 percent and 5.6 percent, respectively. 
The industry benefitted from recovery in manufacturing and public infrastructure 
while the services sector remained buoyant with thriving information technology and 
business process management (IT-BPM) activities24 and domestic tourism25. On the 
other hand, the agriculture sector contracted by 1.5 percent, weighed down by back-
to-back typhoons26 that impacted northern Luzon, coupled with the prevalence of 
animal diseases that disrupted the sector’s value chains during the year.
