5. Agriculture
Energy consumption in the agriculture sector reached 438.8 kTOE in 2024 or 9.3 percent 
higher than its 401.6 kTOE in 2023, reflected a share of 1.1 percent of the country’s 
TFEC. The hike in the sector’s energy use mirrored the shift to mechanized operations, 
expanded livestock farming, as well as resilient harvests that required processing and 
transport and increased use of cold storage and processing facilities.

Energy consumption in the fishery sub-sector went up by 13.2 percent to 193.3 kTOE in 
2024. The uptake was due to increased reliance on cold storage and aeration systems, 
as well as expanded fishing activities in areas recovering from the past climate 
disruptions. Meanwhile, forestry, though a minor component of agricultural energy 
use, increased slightly from 1.0 kTOE to 1.1 kTOE, consistent with small gains in logging 
and wood processing activities in upland areas.
