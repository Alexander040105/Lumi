4. Services12
The Services sector registered a 6.7 percent growth in aggregate value-added and 
outpaced both the industry and agriculture sectors in 2024. It was a major driver 
of economic growth with its significant 62.9 percent share to the overall economy. 
Domestic trade, particularly wholesale and retail13, was buoyed by strong consumer 
demand and increased adoption of e-commerce and digital technologies. Thus, the 
sector’s posted the second-fastest acceleration vis-à-vis other end use sectors at 8.5 
percent to 5.1 MTOE from its 2023 level of 4.7 MTOE.
