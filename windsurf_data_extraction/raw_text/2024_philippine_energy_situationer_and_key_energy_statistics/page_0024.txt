GHG emissions from coal and natural gas by 7.7 percent and 21.1 percent, respectively, 
and accounted for an aggregate share of 64.3 percent of total GHG emissions. Oil-
related emissions rose 3.9 percent, from 55.7 to 57.9 MTCO2e, comprising 35.7 percent 
of the total during the period. (Table 2)
