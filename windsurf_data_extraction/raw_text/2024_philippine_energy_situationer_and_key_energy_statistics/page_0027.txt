ENERGY PER CAPITA.  Per capita energy, electricity, and oil use in 2024 rose to 0.61 
TOE, 1,125.56 kWh, and 1.47 bbl, respectively, and surpassed their 2023 levels. This 
upward trend reflected improved access to energy for both daily needs and productive 
activities, supporting higher living standards and economic participation.
GHG Emission Indicators
Carbon intensity of GDP in 2024 remained relatively at the same level as the previous 
year’s 0.7 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent (tCO₂e) per PHP 100,000. Meanwhile, the carbon 
intensity of TPES and electricity generation increased by 1.4 percent and 0.4 percent, 
respectively. These upticks were largely attributed to the growing share of fossil 
fuels, particularly coal, in the energy and power generation mixes. Consequently, 
GHG emissions per capita also rose by 5.9 percent, in line with higher overall energy 
consumption and emissions output. (Figure 18).
