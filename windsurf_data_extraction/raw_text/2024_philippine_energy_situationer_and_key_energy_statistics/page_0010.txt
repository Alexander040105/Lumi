Petroleum-based fuels remained the primary energy source for the transport sector. 
Demand for gasoline and diesel, with a collective share of 91.1 percent of transport 
energy consumption, went up  by 5.9 percent and 5.1 percent, respectively. This is 
attributed to increased road traffic, higher vehicle sales—particularly of fuel-powered 
cars and delivery trucks—and the continued reliance on diesel for freight and public 
transport operations. Meanwhile, fuel oil use, specifically for domestic maritime 
activities, went up by 5.5 percent in 2024 as operators favored larger, cost-efficient 
vessels amid weather disruptions. Consumption of aviation fuels dropped by 6.0 percent 
due to flight cancellations from severe weather and airline fuel-saving measures.
