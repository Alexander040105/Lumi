A. Oil Refining
The Philippine oil refining sector remained dependent on a single operational facility—
Petron’s Bataan Refinery, albeit 6.5 percent decline in refinery output from 6.5 MTOE 
(47.7 MMB) in 2023 to 6.0 MTOE (50.9 MMB). This reduction stemmed from operational 
challenges and strategic adjustments, as well as scheduled maintenance shutdowns 
that affected the plant’s refinery operations. In addition, the hike in importation of 
finished petroleum products effectively reduced the immediate need for higher 
domestic refining output.
